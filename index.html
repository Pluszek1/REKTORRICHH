The drop model

Runs like a release. Not like a subscription.

Most trading services stay open forever and take anyone. We don't. Each drop is a limited opening — controlled, intentional, and focused on getting real results for a small group of clients.

Questions

FAQ

What is a drop? +
A drop is a limited opening of our offers — a fixed number of spots become available, we accept applications, fill them, and then close. When it's full, it's closed. The next opening is announced separately. Think of it like a limited release from a clothing brand.
Why is it limited? +
Because we only take on clients we can genuinely help. Done-for-you funded passing and 1-on-1 mentorship both require real attention and time. Keeping the numbers low means the quality stays high for everyone in the drop.
What if the drop is already full when I apply? +
You'll be added to the waitlist for Drop 002. Waitlist members get first access when the next drop opens, before any public announcement. Your application carries over — you don't need to reapply.
Which funded account firms do you support? +
We work with all major funded challenge providers. The support is built around the challenge structure and rules of whichever firm you're using. Tell us which firm in your application and we'll confirm fit.
How does the 1-on-1 mentorship work in practice? +
It's fully personalised. We start by reviewing your trading history and identifying where you're losing edge. Then we build a framework specific to you — your markets, your hours, your weaknesses. Sessions are direct and scheduled around your availability.
How do I know if I'm a good fit? +
Apply and be honest about where you are. We're not looking for experienced traders only — we're looking for people who are serious about improving and willing to put in the work. The application is the filter, not your current skill level.
